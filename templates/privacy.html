Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the app, we collect the expense data you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect payment information.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and enabling you to manage your financial records. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party services. We may use infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the app, but they are bound by confidentiality obligations and cannot use your data independently.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the app. We will respond to all privacy-related enquiries within a reasonable timeframe.
